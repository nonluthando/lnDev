Passions

I care about building technology that is inclusive, human-centred, and designed for real-world constraints.

Accessibility

Accessibility is not a feature — it is a design baseline. I care about inclusive systems that work for everyone, including users with disabilities.

  • Inclusive UX, clarity and error prevention
  • Designing for assistive technologies
  • Building software that earns trust

Digital Divide

In South Africa, mobile-first and low-bandwidth design is not optional. I’m interested in building systems that respect data costs, device limitations, and access constraints.

  • Low-bandwidth patterns and progressive enhancement
  • Accessible information design
  • Systems that support underserved communities

Human–Computer Interaction (HCI)

I enjoy designing digital experiences grounded in how people think, learn, and behave. Good UX reduces confusion and prevents mistakes.

  • Affordances, feedback, constraints
  • Usability and user research mindset
  • Human-centred systems

Psychology × Computing

My Psychology background shaped my interest in decision-making and childhood development. I’m fascinated by how information flows through complex systems — brains and computers.

  • Learning, cognition and behaviour
  • Technology for education and development
  • Explainable decision tools
